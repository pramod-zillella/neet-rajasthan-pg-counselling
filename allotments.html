How these fields were matched

The 2026 Rajasthan Board PDF has 1,366 indicative allotment rows. Every registration ID, state-merit rank and AIR agrees with the Board’s 28 October revised merit list. The MCC source is its 24 October final Round 1 result with 28,723 allotments. AIR is unique in both sources; 406 Rajasthan allotment rows have an MCC match. The MCC institution, course and quota shown in those rows are separate allotments, not proof that either seat was joined. One Board row omits its seat-type suffix and is marked “Not stated.”

For 2024, each recovered allotment row was matched to the same-round merit PDF by registration ID; every match agreed on merit number. Domicile, provisional considered category and service status come from that merit row. The 2024 “All India” and 2025–2026 “AI” labels belong to this Rajasthan Board process; they are not MCC AIQ allotments. For 2025, all 5,520 recovered allotment rows were matched to AIR through the round-specific merit PDFs. The recovered 2025 Round 3 PDF remains incomplete. No rank here is a certified final closing cutoff.
