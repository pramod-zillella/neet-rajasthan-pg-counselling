How to interpret these lists

These are academic-session collections: the 2024 exam includes rounds conducted in early 2025, and the 2025 exam includes rounds conducted in early 2026. The 2024 Round 1 rank is labelled combined merit; later rounds use state merit after weightage. Do not relabel those values as NEET-PG AIR.

For 2025, the matching round's merit PDF shows AIR separately. A separate research workbook matched 5,520 recovered allotment records to AIR, but that analysis still cannot establish final joined-seat cutoffs, and the Round 3 allotment copy may be incomplete. This public site provides the original PDFs for direct review.
