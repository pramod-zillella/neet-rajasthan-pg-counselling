How these fields were matched

For 2024, each recovered allotment row was matched to the same-round merit PDF by registration ID; every match agreed on merit number. Domicile, provisional considered category and service status come from that merit row. The 2024 “All India” and 2025 “AI” labels belong to this Rajasthan Board process; they are not MCC AIQ allotments. “Post MBBS DNB/Diploma” in the 2024 seat-type filter is a programme label printed with the institution, not a reservation category.

For 2025, all 5,520 recovered allotment rows were previously matched to AIR through the round-specific merit PDFs; this build rechecked state merit number and AIR for every row. It also adds domicile and provisional considered category from those PDFs. Some long domicile names run into the category column; clear state names were normalized, while the truncated “Dadra and Nagar…” label is marked as such. Some Round 3 dermatology course text collides with the seat-type column; those course labels are normalized to MD Dermatology, Venereology and Leprosy. No rank here is a certified final closing cutoff.
