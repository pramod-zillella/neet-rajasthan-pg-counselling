Read responsibly

What these records can—and cannot—tell you

State merit is not AIR. The 2024 merit PDFs do not explicitly show NEET-PG All India Rank. The 2025 merit PDFs show both, but use the list for the matching round.

An allotment is not a joined seat. These lists are provisional. Round counts overlap, and some later-round records are carried forward.

Check the source before deciding. Original historical portals were unavailable during research; recovered documents came from archived Board PDFs and public mirrors. Later corrigenda may exist.
