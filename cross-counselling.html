Source and matching method

The Rajasthan revised provisional merit list contains 3,165 unique registrations and AIRs. The Rajasthan indicative allotment result contains 1,366 rows; all registration IDs, state ranks and AIRs agree with revised merit. The MCC final Round 1 result contains 28,723 unique AIRs. The supplied MCC CSV omitted 1,733 first rows of PDF pages; this page uses all rows recovered from the official PDF. Source links in the table open the exact PDF page. Candidate names, relatives, gender and roll numbers are excluded from the searchable files.

MCC quotas such as All India, DNB and Self-Financed Merit Seat are MCC allotments. “AI” on a Rajasthan Board seat is a seat type within Rajasthan state counselling, not an MCC allotment. A dash means no matching Round 1 allotment in that source; it does not describe later rounds, reporting or admission.
