Method and limits

2024 merit fields were extracted from the verified archived Board PDFs. For 2025, the AIR and rank values come from previously validated merit research, while domicile and provisional considered category were read from the same-round PDFs. The 2026 Round 1 table is extracted directly from the Board’s 138-page provisional list dated 27 October 2026; all 3,160 list numbers and state-merit numbers were checked. Round lists overlap; counts are records, not distinct applicants or final admissions.

The 2024 domicile field uses the Board’s labels “RAJASTHAN” and “OTHER”; 2025–2026 report named states. The 2026 PDF clips some long domicile names and, on seven Madhya Pradesh rows, puts a stray “h” into “General” in its text layer; unambiguous labels were checked and normalized. Its primary filled reservation category is not printed, so that field remains blank, while the separate additional-category fields are preserved. “NA” bonus and in-service years remain blank; numeric zero remains zero. Five 2024 merit rows have no service label in the source PDF and remain unreported.
