About this collection

The main 2024 Round 1–3, revised Stray, and Special Stray merit PDFs were checked against the Internet Archive file digests and complete printed page sequences. An earlier 28 February 2025 provisional Stray version is linked beside the revised version.

All four 2025 merit PDFs are provisional recovered public copies. Candidate records can recur across rounds; the number of listed entries is not a count of distinct applicants or final admissions. Full source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are available on each document card.
