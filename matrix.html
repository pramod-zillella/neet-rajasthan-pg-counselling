Reading the matrix

In 2024 government-college PDFs, the first seat column is printed “Govt.” in Round 1 and “Recog.” in later rounds; the second is printed “Mgmt.” These are grouped as State (Government) and State (Management) for filtering, while the exact printed label remains in the table and Excel. Private-college “AIQ”, “SQ” and “Mgmt.” columns are grouped as AI, State and Management. The “Total” columns are excluded from row totals to prevent double-counting.

The 2024 DNB PDF also lists Post-MBBS Diploma centres. Those seats have no quota split in the PDF and are labelled Programme total. The 2025 verified matrices cover medical-college MD/MS seats only; do not treat a missing 2025 DNB entry as zero seats.
