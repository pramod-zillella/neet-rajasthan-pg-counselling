Method and limits

The 2025 comparison uses only the revised provisional Round 1 merit list dated 23 November 2025 (3,706 entries). The 2026 comparison uses only the provisional Round 1 list dated 27 October 2026 (3,160 entries). Counts are calculated directly from the site's 2025 merit data and 2026 merit data. Later rounds or revisions can change the list, ranks and eligible pool.

The milestone table includes only records marked Non-Service. At each multiple of 200, it finds the last matching candidate whose state rank is no higher than that milestone; it does not interpolate AIR. Where the milestone itself is In-Service, the matched state rank will be lower. The optional outside-Rajasthan filter excludes “Not Applicable” domicile. Because state merit includes In-Service candidates with bonus marks, AIR is not monotonic across the complete merit list.

“Other domicile” means a named domicile other than Rajasthan. The Board's “Not Applicable” label is separate and is never counted as out-of-state. “Ahead” refers only to order in the published state merit list. In-service bonus marks affect that order. Domicile and service labels alone do not prove which specific seats a candidate can choose. Rajasthan-domicile applicants may also compete for Rajasthan private-college All India/open seats; these are Rajasthan Board seats, not MCC AIQ seats. Use the seat matrix, applicable Board rules and actual allotments to assess a college or branch.
