How to read the comparison

This compares Round 1 seats initially offered, not final sanctioned intake, admissions or seats left after later rounds. The 2024 government/RUHS/society total is 945, 2025 is 980 and the provisional 2026 page 1 total is 990. The full comparable MD/MS totals are 2,008 for 2024 and 2,378 for 2025. The 2024 collection also contains DNB/Diploma, but those programmes are excluded here because a verified 2025 DNB/Diploma matrix is absent.

The Board changed branch wording between 2024 and 2025. Short 2024 names such as “MS (ENT)” are matched to the fuller later label. The two 2024 Anatomy labels and two Transfusion Medicine labels are combined to match the later combined labels. The full CSV and Excel workbook retain source links. “New college” means a college absent from the verified 2024 Round 1 MD/MS matrix; it does not establish the college's actual founding year.
