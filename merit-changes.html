Sources and method

Revised Round 1: 28 October 2026 contains 3,165 records. Provisional Round 2: 20 November 2026 contains 3,413. The supplied Round 2 PDF matches the official Board copy byte-for-byte.

Candidates are matched by unique AIR within the same exam year and cross-checked against registration ID. All shared records retain their registration IDs. “Added” means newly present in the compared list; “absent” would mean no matching record in the other list. Neither establishes the reason or admission status. Candidates remaining on both lists can have corrected attributes or bonus marks; threshold crossings are separated from additions/removals.

State-order counts use the target’s different state rank in each round. Better-AIR counts use AIR strictly below the entered number, irrespective of bonus marks. If the target is absent from a list, its state position is not inferred; the AIR-threshold view remains available. Names, gender and NEET roll numbers are omitted from derived data. Later revisions may change these results.
